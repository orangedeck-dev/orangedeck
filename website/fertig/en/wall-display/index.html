A block height display for the wall

The Clock view shows the current block height in large figures, with fee, price, Moscow time, mempool size and hashrate below it. A bar counts down to the next difficulty adjustment, a line to the halving.

There are no controls on screen, so it reads well from across the room. What stands large in the middle can be changed in the settings, and if several values are chosen they take turns.

On Android the screen stays on while OrangeDeck runs in fullscreen. No permission is needed, and in the background the app keeps nothing awake. The Android build carries a home screen shortcut that opens the clock directly.

Any phone or tablet with Android 9 or newer and a 64-bit ARM processor will do. On a desktop the same view runs as a widget, for example in a corner of a second screen.

Price and Moscow time follow the currency you pick: euro, US dollar, pound, Swiss franc, Canadian or Australian dollar, or yen.

Questions

Does the screen stay on?

Yes, on Android while the app is in fullscreen. When you leave fullscreen or switch apps, the normal screen timeout applies again.

Which tablets work?

Android 9 or newer with a 64-bit ARM processor. That covers most tablets from the last years.

Do I need my own node?

No. The numbers come from mempool.space. If you run your own mempool instance, enter its address in the settings and the clock uses that.
