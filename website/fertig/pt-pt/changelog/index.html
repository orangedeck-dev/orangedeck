Novidades

Todas as versões com a respetiva data, da mais recente para a mais antiga.

As notas de cada versão estão em inglês.

0.2.15 2026-09-26

  • The watch-only wallet is gone, on every platform. OrangeDeck now shows the same five views everywhere and asks for none of your addresses.
  • The service no longer derives addresses from an xpub; a watch list left in sources.json is ignored.

0.2.14 2026-09-26

  • A field in the settings chooses the mempool instance: mempool.space, another public one or your own node. It applies everywhere, including the Android widgets and the service on Linux.
  • The service accepts that change only from the same computer, not from the network and not from a website in a browser.

0.2.13 2026-09-25

  • Numbers and dates follow the language of the interface. English writes dates as 2026-09-25.
  • The fee rate in the details of a mempool tile is calculated from the size and fee shown above it.
  • The price axis in the market view is no longer cut off at the right edge.
  • On Linux the window shows its icon in the title bar and the taskbar.

0.2.12 2026-09-19

  • Android widgets keep their last values when a fetch fails and show the time they are from.
  • A tap on a mempool tile shows its details, a second tap opens the transaction in the explorer.
  • The Android package is 24 MB instead of 57 MB.
  • The settings of the DankMaterialShell plugin speak all thirteen languages.

0.2.11 2026-09-18

  • Android widgets catch up as soon as the network is back.
  • The liquidation view no longer stays blank over long ranges.
  • Tables can be read with the keyboard.

0.2.10 2026-09-16

  • Android widgets draw their last known values immediately.
  • The market shows long and short as soon as the first exchange answers.
  • The comma key opens the settings.

0.2.9 2026-09-14

  • The first Windows build, a ZIP without installation, with desktop widgets.
  • The market works without a service and fetches straight from Binance, Bybit and OKX.
  • The watch-only wallet on phones, tablets and Windows, through a service on another computer.
  • The settings open from a gear button.

0.2.8 2026-09-12

  • The first release, for Android: feed, clock, mining and explorer.
  • Ten home screen widgets for block height, mempool, price, miner and the mining network.
  • The clock in fullscreen keeps the screen on, for an old phone or tablet on the wall.

As versões anteriores, as somas de verificação e as notas de versão completas estão no GitHub.
